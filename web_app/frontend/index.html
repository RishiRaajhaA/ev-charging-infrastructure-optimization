Model Specifications & Problem Definition

Maximal Covering Location Problem (Church & ReVelle, 1974)

Problem & Operational Meaning: Where should we place a limited budget of K high-power charging hubs to maximize existing local stations within driving reach (radius R = 50 km)? Because 70%+ of stations offer only 1 plug, a 50 km radius (~35 min highway drive) acts as an inter-urban reliability anchor, providing high-throughput backup for sparse Level 2 plugs.

Optimality & Realism Boundary: Solved to guaranteed global optimality over the representative candidate set J via branch-and-cut in scipy.optimize.milp (HiGHS). This is explicitly a spatial coverage optimization; it does not fabricate unobserved EV traffic, grid capacity, or land costs.

Regional Infrastructure Parity & Hardware Allocation

Maximizing the minimum connectors-per-station ratio across underserved regions

The Scarcity Bottleneck: Over 70% of stations possess only 1 connector plug, and 5 stations report 0 operational plugs, creating an initial regional floor of exactly 0.0000 CPS. Deploying extra plugs proportionally only widens the chasm between well-funded hubs and deprived corridors.

Max-Min Fairness Formulation: We maximize the lowest regional connectors-per-station ratio t* subject to (Ci + xi) / Si ≥ t* ⇒ Ci + xi ≥ t* × Si and budget constraint ∑ xi = B, where xi ∈ ℤ≥ 0. This mathematically guarantees that every single plug is prioritized to lift the most deprived regions first.

📍 Optimization 1 — Maximum Coverage MIP

Syllabus Category: Specialized Integer Programming (Facility Location / Set Covering)

Maximize: Z = Σ zi
Subject to:
• Σ yj ≤ K   (Hub budget)
• zi ≤ Σj∈Ni yj   ∀ i (Coverage radius)
• yj, zi ∈ {0, 1}

Candidate hubs generated via Spherical K-Means clustering on unit-sphere coordinates.

⚖️ Optimization 2 — Regional Connector Allocation (Max-Min Fairness MILP)

Syllabus Category: Mixed-Integer Linear Programming / Equity & Bottleneck Optimization

Maximize: t
Subject to:
• Ci + xi ≥ t · Si   ∀ region i   (Fairness floor)
• Σ xi = B   (Capital connector budget)
• xi ≥ 0   (Integer plugs),   t ≥ 0   (Continuous)

Lifts the minimum regional connectors-per-station ratio from 0.0000 to 1.2127 with B=500, funding 247 underserved regions.
