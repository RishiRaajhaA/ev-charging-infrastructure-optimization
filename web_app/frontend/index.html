Model Specifications & Problem Definition

Maximal Covering Location Problem (Church & ReVelle, 1974)

Problem: Where should we place a limited budget of $K$ high-power regional charging hubs to maximize the number of existing local stations within driving reach (radius $R$)?

Method: Candidate hub sites are chosen from real station clusters via 3D Spherical K-Means to ensure physical feasibility. The 0-1 Mixed-Integer Program is solved to guaranteed global optimality via branch-and-cut in scipy.optimize.milp.

Resource Allocation & Max-Min Fairness Objective

Raising the floor of the network's most underserved regions

Problem: How should $B$ new connectors be distributed across administrative regions to ensure that the region with the lowest connectors-per-station (CPS) ratio is maximized?

Why Max-Min? Proportional allocation dumps the vast majority of resources into already dense regions. The Max-Min formulation mathematically guarantees that the most neglected regions receive infrastructure first.

Operations Research Formulations & Course Alignment

Mathematical proofs, decision variable definitions, and syllabus mapping

📍 Optimization 1 — Maximum Coverage MIP

Syllabus Category: Specialized Integer Programming (Facility Location / Set Covering)

Maximize: Z = Σ zi
Subject to:
• Σ yj ≤ K   (Hub budget)
• zi ≤ Σj∈Ni yj   ∀ i (Coverage radius)
• yj, zi ∈ {0, 1}

Candidate hubs generated via Spherical K-Means clustering on unit-sphere coordinates.

⚖️ Optimization 2 — Max-Min Fairness MILP

Syllabus Category: Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (Resource Allocation / Sensitivity Analysis)

Maximize: Z = t
Subject to:
• (Ci + xi) / Si ≥ t   ∀ i (Fairness floor)
• Σ xi = B   (Connector budget)
• xi ≥ 0 and integer,   t ≥ 0

Guarantees Pareto-optimal resource allocation elevating the lowest regional ratio.
